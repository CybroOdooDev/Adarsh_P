Screenshots Features Frequent Questions Release Notes

Meeting Subtitles & AI Summaries Settings

In Settings → General Settings, the Meeting Subtitles & AI Summaries section sets the speech-to-text engine, the AI provider and the meeting language.

Speech-to-Text Engines

Choose Whisper or Whistle to transcribe on your own server, or Grok STT or Cloud Whisper for cloud transcription. Add keywords so names and terms are recognized correctly.

Subtitle(CC) Button in the Call Bar

The Subtitle(CC) button is in the call action bar of every Discuss call. Subtitle(CC) is personal: the CC On badge shows that your captions are on. While it is on, the call is also recorded for the meeting summary.

Live Subtitles with Speaker Names

Each caption shows the speaker's name on its own line and stays on screen long enough to read.

Call Transcription

While anyone has Subtitle(CC) on, everyone in the call is notified and sees a Transcribing indicator while the call is recorded into the meeting summary.

Live Meeting Transcript

Open AI Summary & Notes from the call menu to follow the timestamped transcript of the call and copy it.

AI Meeting Summary

Generate the meeting minutes: an executive summary, key discussion points, decisions and action items.

Meeting Minutes Posted to the Channel

Post the summary to the channel with one click. Members can open the full transcript or copy the summary text.

Meeting Summaries List

All meeting summaries are kept under Discuss → Meeting Summaries, with their attendees, AI provider and status.

Meeting Summary Record

Each meeting keeps its summary, key points, decisions, action items and raw transcript, and can be summarized again.

Upload a Meeting Recording

Upload an audio or video recording to a channel to have it transcribed in the background and summarized.

Answers Before You Install

Q
Does meeting audio leave my server?
A
Not with the Whisper and Whistle engines: speech is transcribed on your own Odoo server. Audio is sent out only if you choose a cloud engine (Grok STT or Cloud Whisper) or turn on the OpenAI Speech Fallback.
 
Q
What do I need to install?
A
The local engines need Python packages in the environment that runs Odoo: faster-whisper for Whisper and uploaded recordings, cactus-needle for Whistle. The module installs without them, and Settings warns when the selected engine's package is missing. Grok STT needs no package.
 
Q
Do I need an API key?
A
No for the local engines and the built-in summarizer. Grok STT needs an xAI API key, Cloud Whisper the key of its provider (for example Groq), and OpenAI summaries an OpenAI API key.
 
Q
Which languages are supported?
A
Whisper recognizes 99 languages; Whistle supports English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Polish. Choose the meeting language in Settings, or let it be detected automatically.

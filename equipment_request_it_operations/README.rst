Equipment Request & IT Operation
================================
Employees request software or hardware equipment and report damaged
equipment from one form. Each request is approved by the Department Manager
and then by the HR Officer. Once approved, the Stock Manager creates the
internal transfer that moves the equipment from the source to the destination
location. Damage lines can be turned into employee expenses by the
Department Manager, and every approval step records who approved it and when.

Key Features
------------
* Software or hardware equipment requests with product, description,
  quantity and unit of measure lines.
* Choosing the employee fills in their department and job position.
* Approval workflow: Draft, Waiting for Approval of Department, Waiting for
  Approval of HR, Approved and Equipment Assigned.
* Department Managers, HR Officers and Stock Managers can reject a request.
* The Stock Manager creates a confirmed internal transfer from the request
  lines and can open it from the request.
* Damage details: the unit price comes from the product's sales price and
  the subtotal is computed from the quantity.
* The Department Manager generates one expense per damage line for the
  employee; the expenses are listed on the request and in a smart button.
* Extra Info tab with the creator, the approvers and the approval dates.
* Printable Equipment Report with the approvals, the requested equipment,
  the damages and the expenses.
* Four access levels (Department Manager, HR Officer, Stock Manager and
  Manager) with a menu for each approval queue.

Installation
------------
No external dependencies.

Configuration
-------------
* Go to Settings > Users & Companies > Users and give the users an Equipment
  access level: Department Manager, HR Officer, Stock Manager or Manager
  (which includes the other three).

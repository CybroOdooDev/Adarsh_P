## Module <discuss_meeting_subtitles_ai>

#### 26.10.2026
#### Version 19.0.1.0.0
##### ADD
- Initial commit for Discuss Meeting Live Subtitles & AI Summaries.
- Live subtitles (closed captions) in Discuss calls, transcribed on your own server with Whisper or Whistle, or in the cloud with Grok STT.
- Call transcription into a shared meeting record, with a notice to every participant when it starts.
- AI meeting summaries (built-in summarizer, OpenAI or Ollama) posted to the channel.
- Uploaded meeting recordings transcribed in the background and summarized.

Discuss Meeting Live Subtitles & AI Summaries

Live closed captions and AI meeting minutes for Odoo Discuss calls. Speech is transcribed on your own server by default, so meeting audio never has to leave it.

Key Features

Live Subtitles

Turn on Subtitle(CC) in a call to read what everyone says, with each speaker's name. Captions are personal: only the people who turn them on see them.

Call Transcription

Internal users can turn on Transcribe to record the whole call into a meeting record. Everyone in the call is notified and sees a Transcribing indicator while it runs.

AI Meeting Summaries

Generate meeting minutes with an overview, key discussion points, decisions and action items, during the call or after it, and post them to the channel in one click.

Speech Engines
  • Whisper: accurate local transcription in 99 languages.
  • Whistle: a tiny, fast local model for seven European languages.
  • Auto: Whistle first, with Whisper redoing captions Whistle is unsure of.
  • Grok STT: xAI cloud transcription, with no load on your server.
Summary Providers
  • Built-in summarizer: works without any API key.
  • OpenAI: summaries with models such as gpt-4o-mini.
  • Ollama: a private, local language model.
Recording Upload

Upload an audio or video recording to a channel. It is transcribed in the background, and its summary is posted to the channel when it is ready.

Accurate Names

The names of the people in the call, and the keywords you configure, are passed to the speech engine so names, places and product terms are spelled correctly.

Privacy and Access

Audio stays on your server unless you choose a cloud engine or the OpenAI fallback. Transcripts and summaries are visible to internal members of the channel only.

Languages

Pick the meeting language, or let each speaker's language be detected automatically. A main language is assumed whenever the detection is unsure.

Configuration

  1. Go to Settings > Discuss > Meeting Subtitles & AI Summaries.
  2. Choose the speech-to-text engine. For Grok STT, enter your xAI API key.
  3. Add names, places and product terms to Keywords to improve how they are recognized.
  4. Choose the AI provider for summaries and the speech recognition language.

How to Use

  1. Start or join a call in any Discuss channel.
  2. Click Subtitle(CC) to see live captions, and Transcribe to record the conversation.
  3. Open AI Summary & Notes to read the transcript and generate a summary.
  4. Post the summary to the channel, or find it later under Discuss > Meeting Summaries.

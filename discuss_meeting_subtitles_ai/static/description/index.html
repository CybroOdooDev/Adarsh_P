Key Highlights

Community & Enterprise Support

Works seamlessly in both Odoo Community and Enterprise editions.

Live Subtitles in Discuss Calls

Read what everyone says during audio and video calls, with each speaker's name next to the text.

Call Transcription

Record the whole call into a meeting record. Everyone in the call is notified while it runs.

AI Meeting Summaries

Turn the transcript into minutes with key points, decisions and action items, and post them to the channel.

Local or Cloud Speech Engines

Transcribe on your own server with Whisper or Whistle, or in the cloud with Grok STT or Cloud Whisper (Groq, OpenAI or your own GPU server).

Accurate Names and Terms

Participant names and your keywords are passed to the speech engine, so they are spelled correctly.

Recording Upload

Upload an audio or video recording to a channel: it is transcribed in the background and summarized.

Caption Failure Notices

When a caption cannot be transcribed, the speaker sees a short notice saying why, such as a reached request limit.

The local speech engines use external python dependencies: faster-whisper for Whisper and the transcription of uploaded recordings, and cactus-needle for Whistle. Install them in the python environment that runs Odoo before using these engines, with the following commands. The module installs without them, and the cloud engines (Grok STT and Cloud Whisper) need no package for live captions.

pip install faster-whisper cactus-needle

Alternatively, you can use:

pip3 install faster-whisper cactus-needle

If you are using Odoo.sh, you can install the required packages by following the instructions in the blog post below:

https://www.cybrosys.com/blog/how-to-install-an-external-python-package-on-odoosh

Meeting Subtitles & AI Summaries Settings

Go to Settings > Discuss to choose the speech engine, the Whisper model, keywords, the meeting language and the AI summarization engine.

Subtitles, Transcription and AI Summary in the Call

In a Discuss call, open the More menu to turn on Subtitle(CC), start transcribing, or open AI Summary & Notes.

Live Captions

Each caption shows the speaker's name. New captions appear under the earlier ones, which stay on screen long enough to be read.

Call Transcription

When transcription starts, everyone in the call is notified and sees the Transcribing indicator.

Live Meeting Transcript

AI Summary & Notes shows the timestamped transcript of the call so far, ready to copy.

AI Meeting Summary

Generate the summary with key discussion points, decisions and action items, then post it to the channel.

Meeting Minutes in the Channel

The summary is posted to the channel with the date, duration and attendees, and a link to the full transcript.

Meeting Summaries

Every meeting is kept under Discuss > Meeting Summaries, with its attendees and status.

Meeting Summary Record

Each record holds the summary, the key points and decisions, the action items and the raw transcript.

Upload a Meeting Recording

Upload an audio or video recording from the channel: it is transcribed in the background and its summary is posted when it is ready.

Notice When a Caption Is Lost

If a caption cannot be transcribed, for example when a cloud engine's request limit is reached, the speaker is told why.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does meeting audio leave my server?

Not with the Whisper and Whistle engines: speech is transcribed on your own Odoo server. Audio is sent out only if you choose a cloud engine (Grok STT or Cloud Whisper) or turn on the OpenAI Speech Fallback.

What do I need to install?

The local engines need Python packages in the environment that runs Odoo: faster-whisper for Whisper and uploaded recordings, cactus-needle for Whistle. The module installs without them, and Settings warns when the selected engine's package is missing. Grok STT needs no package.

Do I need an API key?

No for the local engines and the built-in summarizer. Grok STT needs an xAI API key, Cloud Whisper the key of its provider (for example Groq), and OpenAI summaries an OpenAI API key.

Who sees the captions?

Subtitle(CC) is personal: only the participants who turn it on see captions. While anyone has it on, everyone in the call is captioned.

Who can read the transcripts and summaries?

Internal users who are members of the channel. Only internal users can turn transcription on or off.

Which languages are supported?

Whisper recognizes 99 languages; Whistle supports English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Polish. Choose the meeting language in Settings, or let it be detected automatically.

How quickly do captions appear?

A caption appears after the speaker pauses. With a cloud engine it typically takes about one to two seconds; with a local engine it depends on the server's CPU, or GPU if it has one.

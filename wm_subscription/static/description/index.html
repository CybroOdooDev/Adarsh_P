How does weight quota tracking work with collection orders?
When collection orders linked to a subscribed partner are weighed and marked done, the confirmed weight of completed or signed orders in the current month-long window (counted from the subscription start date) is summed and shown on the subscription as collected weight, remaining quota and a quota-exceeded flag. The allowance is applied when the collections are invoiced (Monthly Billing Run, consolidated or per-order invoices).
Can customers see their subscription in the portal?
Yes. The customer portal lists their subscriptions and shows plan, price, billing cycle, start, end and next invoice dates, auto-renew, contract and covered sites, with cancellation and one-click PDF download. Quota usage is not shown in the portal.
How does renewal invoicing automation work?
A daily scheduled action (03:00) renews active auto-renew subscriptions whose next invoice date has arrived (a manager can also press Renew Billing Cycle). Each renewal creates a draft customer invoice for the recurring fee only. Weight beyond the allowance is invoiced with the collections, and the extra pickup subscriber discount is applied to the extra pickup charge; these are separate from the recurring-fee invoice.
Does it integrate with standard Odoo Invoicing and Accounting?
Yes. Generated invoices are standard account.move records with standard journal entries, payment links, and fiscal localization support. A chart of accounts (or a default income account on the Sales journal) is required; otherwise a clear message explains what to set up.

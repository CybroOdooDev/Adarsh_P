==============================
Waste Management Subscriptions
==============================
Recurring customer subscription plans, weight quota allowances and automatic billing for Waste Management.

* Reusable catalog of subscription plans: Monthly, Quarterly and Annually.
* Subscriber benefits: weight quota allowances, container swap quotas, extra pickup discounts and SLA levels.
* Automatic generation of recurring cycle invoices.
* Subscription renewal management linked to the customer contract.

Configuration
=============
* Define the plans and their benefits under ``Waste Management > Configuration > Customer > Subscription Plans``.
* Create a subscription for a customer with a signed or confirmed contract and activate it.
